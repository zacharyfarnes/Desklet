Privacy Policy

Private by design.

Effective 30 October 2026

Overview

Desklet does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. The app does not require an account and does not contain analytics, advertising or tracking technologies.

Notes and preferences

Notes and preferences created in Desklet are stored locally on your device. Desklet does not operate a server or cloud service and does not receive the content you enter.

Your device and operating-system services may manage local backups according to your own device settings. Those services are provided and controlled by their respective platform providers.

Third-party services

Desklet does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs or social integrations.

Support enquiries

If you contact us for support, the information you provide is used only to respond to your request. Email is handled by the email services used by you and by the developer.

Changes to this policy

If Desklet’s privacy practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect. The effective date at the top of this page will show the latest revision.

Contact
