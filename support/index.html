Frequently asked questions

Where are my notes stored?

Your notes are stored locally on your device. Desklet has no account, backend or cloud-sync service.

How do I edit or delete a note?

Tap a note to edit it. To delete a note, touch and hold it, then choose Delete from the context menu.

How do I reorder notes?

Touch and drag a note to move it. Your chosen order is saved and also used in Desk Mode.

Why does the screen stay awake in Desk Mode?

Desk Mode is intended to remain visible while your device is on a desk or stand, so it temporarily prevents automatic display sleep. Normal display-sleep behaviour returns when Desk Mode closes or the app becomes inactive.

How does automatic Desk Mode work on iPhone Duo?

On supported iPhone Duo devices, Desklet can enter Desk Mode when the device is placed in an appropriate folded posture and leave it when the device unfolds. Manual Desk Mode remains available on every supported device.

Can I recover a deleted note?

No. Desklet is designed for temporary, current information and does not include an archive or trash folder.
