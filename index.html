Designed for your desk

From colourful notes to a calm display.

Desk Mode transforms your notes into large, readable cards that work beautifully in portrait or landscape. Place your device on a stand and keep useful information beside you while you work.

Simple notes

Just enough structure.

Write freely, choose a colour, and move notes into the order that makes sense to you. There are no folders, tags or systems to maintain.

01

Simple by design

One free-form text field, five friendly colours, and straightforward editing. Nothing to organise before you can write.

02

Arrange it your way

Drag notes into the order that makes sense to you. Desk Mode respects the same arrangement.

03

Made to stay visible

Desk Mode keeps the screen awake while active, with vertical or horizontal scrolling and an optional date and time.

Thoughtful on every device

Especially at home on iPhone Duo.

On supported iPhone Duo devices, Desklet can enter Desk Mode automatically when the device is placed in an appropriate folded posture—and return when it unfolds.

Private by design

Your notes are yours.

Desklet requires no account and collects no personal data. Notes and preferences remain stored locally on your device.
